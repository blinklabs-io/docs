## v0.202.8 (September 4, 2026)

**Title:** Reject duplicate members in encoded sets

**Date:** September 4, 2026

**Version:** v0.202.8

This release makes duplicate-member validation consistent for Conway and Dijkstra transaction and witness decoding, preventing CBOR-encoded untagged arrays with duplicate members from being accepted or deferred.

### 💪 Improvements

* Improved **duplicate-member validation:** Conway and Dijkstra now reject CBOR-encoded untagged arrays containing duplicate members during decoding. The check covers transaction bodies, witness sets, guards, invalid-transaction indices, and proposal-procedure sets, including Dijkstra vkey witnesses that previously could pass unchecked.
