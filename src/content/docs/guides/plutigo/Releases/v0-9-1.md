## v0.9.1

**Title:** Accelerate full-corpus evaluation

**Version:** v0.9.1

**Release:** [v0.9.1](https://github.com/blinklabs-io/plutigo/releases/tag/v0.9.1)

This release accelerates full-corpus Plutigo evaluation, improves FLAT decoding, restores complete use-case benchmark coverage, and adds an optional profiling workflow.

### Performance

* Improved CEK evaluation throughput with faster indexing, budget and arity handling, variable dispatch, environment lookup, and loop control.
* Optimized FLAT decoding with faster 64-bit and four-bit processing paths.

### Benchmarks

* Restored the complete use-case benchmark corpus with all 89 scripts.

* Added the opt-in `make bench-pgo` workflow and `tests/use_cases.pgo` profile data, with usage documented in the README.
