### Dashboard, governance, and monitoring improvements

* Improved [dashboard health status](https://github.com/blinklabs-io/dingo/pull/4744) by basing sync classifications on tip gap and recent slot advance rate, evaluating status across the selected time range, and showing `UNKNOWN` when metrics are unavailable. Startup samples are skipped and earlier status signals remain available in tooltips, reducing false `SYNCED` and `STALLED` states during genesis sync.
