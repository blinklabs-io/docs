## v0.73.3

### Dashboard, governance, and monitoring improvements

* Improved [dashboard health status](https://github.com/blinklabs-io/dingo/pull/4744) by basing sync classifications on tip gap and recent slot advance rate, evaluating status across the selected time range, and showing `UNKNOWN` when metrics are unavailable. Startup samples are skipped and earlier status signals remain available in tooltips, reducing false `SYNCED` and `STALLED` states during genesis sync.
* Corrected [Conway governance expiry handling](https://github.com/blinklabs-io/dingo/pull/4676) so final boundary ratification runs before expiration, already ratified proposals stay out of expiry processing, DReps remain active through the expiry epoch, and descendant and orphan handling uses the correct prior epoch proposal set.
* Added [recent block delay metrics](https://github.com/blinklabs-io/dingo/pull/4742) for the last 16 blocks at the chain tip. The history keeps each delay paired with its block number and ignores empty, repeated, or out of order deliveries, allowing operators to chart delay spikes without mismatched data.
